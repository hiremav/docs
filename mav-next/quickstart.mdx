This guide walks you through your first day in Mav Next - from logging in to watching your first lead get worked.

## Tour the workspace

After logging in, you'll find these areas on the home page:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Contacts" icon="contact-round" href="/mav-next/agents-overview">
    Everyone Mav is working, with full conversation history.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Marketing Sources" icon="star" href="/mav-next/contacts-overview">
    Route a vendor's leads to one or many agents. 
  </Card>

  <Card title="Import Leads or Conversions" icon="import" href="/mav-next/marketing-sources">
    Upload a CSV of your leads for your agent to work.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Invite users" icon="mail-plus" href="/mav-next/account-and-transfer-hours">

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Get your first lead flowing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your agent">
    Partner with your Client Success leader to set up for your account’s agents (for example, Auto Insurance and/or Home Insurance). Check its [persona and instructions](/mav-next/configuring-an-agent) so it sounds like your brand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team ">
    Add users to your Mav account 
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a lead source">
    Set up a [marketing source](/mav-next/marketing-sources) so your vendor or web form can deliver leads straight to the agent, or [import a CSV](/mav-next/importing-contacts) to load a batch yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your availability">
    Configure [transfer hours](/mav-next/account-and-transfer-hours) so live calls are only attempted when your reps can actually take them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it work">
    As leads arrive, open **Contacts** to follow each [conversation](/mav-next/contacts-overview) in real time and review outcomes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
