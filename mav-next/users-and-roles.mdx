Bring your team into Mav and control what each person can do. Manage your roster under **Settings → Users**.

## Managing users

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite a teammate">
    Add a user by email from **Settings → Users**. They'll get an invitation email and set their own password on acceptance — it must meet Mav.next's [password requirements](/mav-next/signing-in-and-account-security#password-requirements).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set their access">
    Assign the appropriate role so each person has the access they need — and nothing they don't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update or remove">
    Adjust a teammate's access or remove them at any time as your team changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Every Mav sign-in is also covered by an automatic [account lockout](/mav-next/signing-in-and-account-security#login-lockout) after too many failed attempts. If a teammate can't get in, point them at that page.
</Note>
